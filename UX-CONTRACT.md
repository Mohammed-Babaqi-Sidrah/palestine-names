Table Selection, CRUD and Toast are not applicable: the public archive is read-only. Search dates remain available via the API; this surface offers name and exact-age filters, so no calendar is needed. Rendering silhouettes uses a labeled sample, with complete paginated records as the accessible equivalent.

Departures converge on the exact tiny, evenly distributed olive-grey figures behind «04 — سِجل الذاكرة الحيّة». Share positions, .025 scale and the quiet palette with the archivist. Complete forms retain their approved colours; departure fades to quiet ink, uploads only when that blend changes and skips already-quiet modes. Preserve progression, gathering, settled-only picking, non-interactive archivist, stopped idle work and resource limits. The finale retains its original centre until the footer approaches, then lifts above it and clips at its top edge; hide as it passes the masthead. The footer is excluded from picking. Logo/source identity stays aligned on a single row down to 320px; columns stack, and the return link shares the bottom motto row.

The opening number retains its dark flag inks (#a52b28, #1c6840, #bbb7ad, #151a16) and reduced paper wash. The finale uses the original flag palette (#e4312b, #149954, #fff, #1e221e) and default paper wash. Neither adds a glow. The remembrance sentence is exactly «هؤلاء مَن وُثِّقَت أسماؤهم… وليسوا كلَّ مَن فقدنا.» in deep-red Amiri in the navbar, with responsive wrapping, no drop and no duplicate finale note.

Phone interaction: at <=1000px, age categories use 44px targets separate from the accurately proportional decorative track. The same buttons retain Arabic names, percentages, pressed state and selected-cohort feedback. Source/brand/social links meet 44px targets; assistant input uses at least 16px text. Portrait and landscape navigation, long record dialogs and contained Markdown/code overflow must remain usable. Responsive footer clipping checks wait for the actual mask update after viewport/scroll synchronization, rather than using the changing centre alone as a readiness proxy.
